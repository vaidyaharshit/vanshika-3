This engine implements the latest Indian Direct Tax provisions for Financial Year 2024-25 / 2025-26 (Assessment Year 2025-26 / 2026-27):

• New Tax Regime (Sec 115BAC): ₹75,000 Standard Deduction, Sec 87A rebate covers income up to ₹7,00,000 (Tax = ₹0).

• Health & Education Cess: 4% applicable on total tax after rebate across both regimes.
